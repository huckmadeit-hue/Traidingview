About Reserve EV

A five-car fleet, run like it matters.

Reserve EV is an independently operated Tesla fleet in the Raleigh–Durham area — small enough that every car gets real attention, deliberate enough that every trip starts the same way: clean, charged, inspected, and ready.

What we believe

The preparation between trips is as important as the vehicle itself. Anyone can list a car online. Operating a fleet means the unglamorous work — the inspection after every return, the detail clean, the recharge, the settings reset — happens every single time, documented.

Technology should make travel more enjoyable, not more intimidating. A Tesla is genuinely easier to live with than most cars — once someone shows you the three things that are different. So we show you, in plain English, before you drive.

Hospitality scales down well. With five vehicles, you get modern electric driving with an actual human paying attention — answering questions, making the handoff easy, and caring how the trip went.
