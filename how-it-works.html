How it works

From browsing to the open road.

Reserve EV is where you choose the car and get comfortable with it. Turo is where the reservation itself happens. Here’s the whole journey.

  1. Step 1 · Browse

    Browse the five Teslas

    Compare seats, cargo, range, and best-fit trips on the Fleet page — or use the Trip Fit selector on the homepage to start from your trip instead of the car.

  2. Step 2 · Select

    Pick the vehicle that fits

    Each vehicle page lays out verified specs, first-timer notes, and honest best-use advice. Still torn? Ask us — a human replies.

  3. Step 3 · Continue to Turo

    See live pricing and availability

    The “Check Availability on Turo” button takes you to that vehicle’s Turo listing, where dates, pricing, and protection plans are always current.

  4. Step 4 · Reserve

    Complete the reservation on Turo

    Turo handles identity verification, payment, eligibility, and trip protection. Reserve EV never takes payment on this site.

  5. Step 5 · Get oriented

    Receive pickup & Tesla guidance

    Before your trip you’ll get clear pickup information and a plain-English Tesla orientation — especially useful for first-timers. Exact pickup locations and any delivery options are being finalized; your trip’s Turo listing has the current details.

  6. Step 6 · Drive & return

    Enjoy the trip, return as confirmed

    Drive, charge, explore. At the end of the trip, follow the return instructions confirmed for your reservation — then we start the Ready Standard turnaround for the next guest.

The part you don’t see

Between every trip: the Ready Standard

Inspected, cleaned, recharged, ready — a documented four-step turnaround on every vehicle, every time. It’s why a Reserve EV car feels the same on your fifth trip as it did on your first.
