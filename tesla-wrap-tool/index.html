Tesla Custom Wrap Logo Tool

Place your logo on official Tesla wrap templates for Model Y (2023), Model Y (2024) and Model 3 (2025), then export spec-compliant PNGs. Everything runs locally in this browser tab — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your logo and placements are saved automatically so you can close this file and come back later.

1. Logo

Click or drag & drop your logo here
PNG, SVG, or JPG — transparency recommended

2. Vehicle

Tesla ships two Model 3 (2024+) templates. Pick the trim that matches your car — confirm this is right before exporting.

3. Placement

Add a copy for each body panel you want the logo on (hood, doors, etc.), then drag each one into place. "Mirror" clones the selected copy to the opposite side of the car.
Upload a logo to begin.

4. Export

Letters, numbers, underscores, dashes, spaces only. Final filename (incl. ".png") is kept to 30 characters.

Loading these wraps onto your car

Mobile app (v4.59.0+): Creations → Wrap → Upload.

USB drive: put the exported PNGs in a folder named Wraps on the drive, plug it in, then in the car go to Toybox → Paint Shop → Wraps tab.

Templates sourced from Tesla's official teslamotors/custom-wraps repository. This tool is unofficial and not affiliated with Tesla.
