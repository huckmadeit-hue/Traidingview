Booking

You browse and compare vehicles here, then complete the reservation on the vehicle’s Turo listing. Turo handles live pricing, availability, driver eligibility, payment, and protection plans. Reserve EV’s website never takes payment, and submitting our contact form does not hold a vehicle or confirm a reservation.

Availability is live on Turo. Open the vehicle’s page here and use “Check Availability on Turo” to see your exact dates and pricing.

Pricing is set and displayed on each Turo listing and can vary by dates and trip length, so we don’t publish prices here that could go stale. The Turo listing always shows the current number.

Cancellations and changes are handled through Turo under the terms shown on your trip. Reserve EV’s own cancellation notes will be published here once finalized.
